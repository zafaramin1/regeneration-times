Privacy Notice

Effective October 27, 2026 · Version 2026-10-27 · Current personal-use service

Your choices have separate purposes. Reading the journal, saving a care desk, submitting a form and asking AI to draft an inquiry are different actions. Saving is not permission for AI review or publication.

1. Scope and operator

This notice describes the current Regeneration Times public journal and its operator-only private account tools, operated by Zafar Amin. It is not a promise about a future commercial service. The private tools are not currently available for general public patient accounts.

2. Reading and entries on the public page

The journal downloads public article and resource files. Search and questionnaire matching run in your browser. Generic topic filters and search words may appear in a shareable URL; anyone receiving that link can read them. Avoid putting private details in the search box or a shared URL.

Questionnaire answers, optional context, notes and timeline stay in page memory until you deliberately connect account saving. Without that connection they may be lost on reload or closing. Saved article IDs use local storage on this browser. Search links do not transfer personal notes or saved reading.

Inquiry drafts, recipients, manually recorded statuses and follow-up dates stay in page memory unless you select Remember for this browser. That optional inquiry history is stored unencrypted in browser local storage and may be visible to anyone using the same browser profile. Turning Remember off removes that stored copy; clearing the inquiry log removes the app’s page and browser copy. Exported files remain separate.

3. Optional private account saving

After sign-in and your storage choice, the journal sends questionnaire answers, interests, notes, timeline and saved-story IDs to the private save service. It stores an encrypted account-linked copy in the site’s cloud database. The hosting service uses your account identity to separate records. The application encrypts stored workspace contents with a server-held key; this is not end-to-end encryption, and authorized server processing can decrypt them.

The private workspace also supports saving a profile, provider shortcuts and inquiries there. Its manual Save to my account is separate from the public journal connection. Public-journal inquiry history does not automatically transfer. An authorized operator and the hosting, authentication and storage providers may process information as needed to run and support the service; the owner-only interface is not a claim that no provider can access data.

Successful saves also keep encrypted, dated versions with document names in account history. Restoring an earlier version changes the working page; subsequent edits are saved as a new document. Replacing the latest copy does not erase older versions. The journal’s save connection uses a small private window. Automatic saves stop if it closes or an error occurs. Successful saved entries remain in the account and can be restored after sign-in. Do not close while a save is pending.

4. AI inquiry drafting

Before generation, the private AI helper displays the selected source and question and requests separate consent to send them to OpenAI. The model receives that displayed information. Saved profiles, documents, unrelated care notes and the complete inquiry history are not attached automatically. Do not include information you lack authority to share.

Requests use the API’s store:false setting. That setting is not a promise of zero retention, zero logging or a special health-data agreement; provider retention and processing rules still apply. Review OpenAI’s API data information. Outputs can be wrong. The helper cannot send email. A synthetic connection check uses fixed example text rather than patient records.

5. Forms, document submissions and community choices

Signup, ideas and story forms send the fields you submit to the operator through Google Forms and a private response workbook. The public page explains the requested fields and consent. Story publication requires your separate publication choice; private feedback is not authorization to publish it. Do not assume a submission succeeded unless Google confirms it.

A separate Google document-upload form requires Google sign-in and its own storage and editor-access consent. It accepts one PDF up to 10 MB. Google associates the uploader’s account information with the submission, as disclosed on the form. Files are stored in the operator’s restricted Google Drive upload location. A file upload does not authorize AI review or public use. Redact unnecessary identifiers and upload only when authorized. The document route is not a provider patient portal.

6. Microsoft, providers and other platforms

The private workspace may open a OneDrive folder shortcut. This website does not currently read or upload OneDrive records automatically. ChatGPT connector actions and files you upload directly to Microsoft are separate from the website’s account database. Provider-portal links are bookmarks, not a connection to the records behind them. Do not enter portal passwords here.

GitHub Pages hosts the public journal. ChatGPT Sites, its authentication service and Cloudflare infrastructure host the private service and database. Google handles submitted forms; OpenAI processes explicitly authorized AI requests; Microsoft handles files you separately place there. Those providers and linked sites may process technical details such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, sign-in and security logs under their own practices. Embedded services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. This app does not add advertising trackers or sell care-desk contents.

7. Retention, access, corrections and deletion

Page-only entries last until cleared, reloaded or closed. Device-local saved reading and optional inquiry history remain until removed from the app or browser storage. Account workspaces and submitted forms currently have no automatic expiry schedule. They are retained until removed or an appropriate deletion request is fulfilled. Providers may keep operational or backup copies under their own schedules; this notice does not promise immediate removal from all backups.

Edit and save your account entries to correct them. Clear personal entries on the journal clears the page copy; when connected, a subsequent save can replace the saved journal contents. It does not delete earlier dated versions, a separate private profile, inquiry log, Google submission, OneDrive file or downloaded backup. Request deletion of account history separately if you want earlier versions removed. Use the separate controls for each location or request help from the operator.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, use Have an idea? and choose private feedback. Identify the relevant feature or submission without including medical records. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights. The operator may need to confirm your identity or authority. Do not use this route for urgent medical needs.

8. Security and the limits of this notice

Current application safeguards include authenticated private access, encrypted stored workspaces and checks that prevent stale saves from silently overwriting newer copies. Backups and browser-local inquiry history have different protections. No independent security audit, HIPAA certification or guarantee of absolute security is claimed. Protect your account and device, and use a private browser profile.

This is an informational organization tool, not a healthcare provider or an electronic medical-record service offered on a provider’s behalf. Do not assume HIPAA applies or that this notice replaces legal obligations. Applicable privacy, security and breach-notification duties depend on the service and law.

9. Acknowledgments and updates

For private tools, the service records your account identifier, the notice/terms version and the server timestamp when you affirm the informational scope and accept the Terms of Use. This acknowledgment is separate from storage consent, AI-processing consent and publication choices. No diagnosis or medical record is needed to record it. Reading public articles does not require agreeing to store health information.

Material changes will be identified and, where appropriate, presented for a new acknowledgment or consent before a new use. Before public patient accounts or paid services are offered, the operator will need to update these notices and the supporting controls.
