4. Saving and optional backups

Cloud saving works only when the journal is connected to the private save window. Wait for a successful Saved status; an open page or a pending save is not confirmation. When you return, connect and restore your saved care desk. Successful saves keep named, dated versions in account history. Replacement makes the journal entries current without merging them; earlier versions remain. You can instead save an additional dated document. Restoring an older version replaces unsaved page entries, and later edits create a new document. Inquiry history has a separate device-storage option and does not automatically become part of the journal cloud save.

Backups are optional. Downloaded backups, copied notes and inquiry logs can contain health information and are not encrypted by this app. Keep them private. The service cannot recall files, messages or calendar events you have saved or shared elsewhere.
